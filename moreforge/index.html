How lock duration and optional burns build power

Principal + duration bonus + burned MORE

The duration bonus rises from zero at 8 days to 100% of principal at 1,000 days. Each extra MORE burned adds one unit of power, up to 3× principal. There is no amount threshold or separate Foundry mint.
