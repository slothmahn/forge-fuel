PROPOSED

Amount bonus grows from 1× to 2×, reaching its maximum at 1,000 MORE burned. Term bonus grows from 1× at 8 days to 2.5× at 1,000 days. Burn 1,000 MORE for 1,000 days to reach exactly 5× power. You can burn more than 1,000 MORE: total power keeps growing, but the multiplier never exceeds 5×.
