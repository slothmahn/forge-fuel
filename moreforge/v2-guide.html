Both chains are open. This guide describes the current MORE Forge rules. Earlier contracts follow different rules; V1 positions and rewards remain accessible here.

Choose your principal and optional burn

Lock a positive amount of MORE for 8–1,000 days. You can lock only, or permanently burn extra MORE up to three times your locked principal. Optional burned MORE goes to the dead address and is never returned. The position NFT carries your locked principal and reward rights.

How power works

Power equals principal, plus a duration bonus, plus the extra MORE burned. The duration bonus increases linearly from zero at 8 days to 100% of principal at 1,000 days, rounded down in token base units.

For example, locking 100 MORE and burning 300 extra MORE for 1,000 days gives 500 power. Only the 100 MORE principal can be returned. Locking the same 100 MORE without burning gives 200 power at 1,000 days. Power is a share weight, not a promised return.

Withdraw your principal on time

There is no early withdrawal. At maturity and for 7 days afterward, the full principal is withdrawable. During the following 7 days, unclaimed principal and power decline linearly to zero. Withdrawn principal is sent to the position owner; forfeited principal is sent to the dead address when withdrawal or expiry finalization closes the position. Anyone may finalize a fully decayed position; the caller cannot take its principal.

Power remains full through maturity and the grace period unless the position is closed first. Withdrawing ends future power and burns the position NFT. Previously earned rewards remain claimable by the recorded final owner. A transfer before closure gives the recipient principal and all unclaimed and future reward rights.

Principal-based protocol fee

The fee uses the main MORE liquidity pair’s on-chain spot value of the locked principal. Optional burns and lock duration do not add a fee. There is no price averaging. Market prices in the header are USD references; the contract quote determines payment.

One position, four payout pools

Every position can participate in all four pools while it has eligible power at the cycle deadline. There is no separate Foundry mint. A position that has closed or fully decayed before a deadline cannot earn that cycle. A position created at the exact deadline is excluded. Short locks do not automatically qualify for a distant 288-day deadline.

Bitcoin bought during entry

The Bitcoin allocation is swapped during the same entry transaction. If the required quote or swap fails, the whole entry reverts, including principal movement, optional burn and fee routing. Conversion applies minimum-output checks, a per-swap cap and a maximum of 32 chunks. Actual output depends on liquidity and execution prices.

Cycle timing, settlement and claims

The verified anchor on both chains is October 3, 2026 at 17:00 UTC. Launching before that anchor gives the first cycles a few extra hours. The four first deadlines are 8, 28, 88 and 288 days after the anchor; later cycles repeat at fixed UTC. This is 1 PM Eastern during daylight saving and noon Eastern during standard time. The site displays your local dates and times.

After a cycle closes, anyone can trigger settlement and receive a 0.25% caller reward. Up to 15 position records per pool are processed by the site’s four-pool batch; large snapshots need additional calls. Up to 20 reward records can be claimed together. Rewards do not auto-transfer to wallets, and already-earned claims do not expire.

Buy-and-burn pools and owner controls

Each token receives a fixed 5% of protocol fees. Eligible burns use 10-minute intervals. Anyone can execute a burn and receive 1.5% of its execution amount in native coin. Gas can exceed that incentive. Only the owner controls daily drip (1–10%), swap caps and bounded slippage. Owner entry pauses do not block existing withdrawals, transfers, settlements or claims.

Market and contract limits

Funding, prices and competing power change. Returns are not guaranteed. Spot prices can move sharply or be manipulated. Transactions can fail when liquidity or minimum-output checks cannot be met. MORE Forge has local and chain-fork tests and deployment bytecode and live function checks on both chains; it has not received an independent audit.
